related: [weak-methodology.md, dataset-quality.md]

A strong methodology section should have:
- Clear research question stated before method choice
- Justified sample size (statistical power or precedent cited)
- Baseline comparisons (not just absolute numbers)
- Reproducibility: hyperparameters, seeds, hardware reported
- Ablation studies showing each component's contribution
- Independent test set not used during development
