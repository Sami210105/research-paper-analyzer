Watch for these red flags when evaluating a methodology:
- No control group or baseline comparison
- Sample size under 30 with no statistical justification
- Correlation presented as causation
- Evaluation only on the training distribution
- Hyperparameters not reported
- Results not reproducible from the paper alone
- Method compared only against outdated baselines
