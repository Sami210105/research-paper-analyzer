Methodology is the section of a research paper that explains HOW the study was conducted.

Key components:
- Research design (experimental, observational, simulation, survey)
- Data collection method and source
- Model or algorithm used and why it was chosen
- Evaluation protocol (train/test split, cross-validation, benchmarks)
- Statistical tests used
