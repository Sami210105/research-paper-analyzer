The dataset is the data used to train, validate, or evaluate the method.

Key questions to ask about any dataset:
- Is it publicly available or proprietary?
- What is the size (number of samples)?
- Is it balanced or imbalanced across classes/groups?
- Is it a standard benchmark or a custom collected dataset?
- What is the time period of data collection?
