Limitations are constraints on the validity or generalizability of the findings.

Types of limitations:
- Scope limitations: method only tested on one domain or dataset
- Data limitations: small sample, biased collection, lack of diversity
- Computational limitations: method too expensive to scale
- Reproducibility limitations: missing hyperparameters, proprietary data
- Generalizability: results may not hold outside the experimental setup

A paper that honestly states limitations is more trustworthy than one that does not.
